# Legal notes (not legal advice)

Checklist before any public release or sale:

1. IP lawyer review of the clean-room process records, naming, and traces.
2. Similarity audit: token-level scan of the Rust code against the public
   decomp; rewrite flagged regions from the spec; keep the records.
3. Confirm golden traces may ship publicly (numbers only) or keep them
   private with published hashes.
4. Confirm the wiki sources' license/terms allow the transcribed anchors,
   with attribution and revision ids.
5. Neutral naming everywhere user-visible: no Nintendo names or marks in
   crate names, node classes, docs, or product name.
